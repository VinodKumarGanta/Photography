Behind the lens

Hi, I'm Alex.

In an age where thousands of digital photographs are buried in smartphones and hard drives, I longed for the permanence of a real, physical image. A photograph you could hold, pin to a wall, or slip into a wallet.

That's how LensCraft Instant Photography was born. I've spent the last decade collecting and mastering vintage polaroid cameras and pairing them with cutting-edge mobile dye-sublimation printers. Now, my team and I roam events, capturing the raw, unfiltered joy, and putting the memories right into your guests' hands before the night is over.
